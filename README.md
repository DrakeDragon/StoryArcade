# DrakeDragon’s Story Arcade

**Nine worlds. Your story.**

A collection library for nine interactive LitRPG stories and browser games. Step through a looking glass, rewrite reality, climb a cosmic dungeon, forge legendary equipment, or build an empire from a puddle. Every adventure brings its own world, characters, choices, and progression systems.

The Story Arcade gives the collection a single starting point: a cinematic, responsive menu with illustrated game cards, descriptions, highlights, and direct links to each adventure.

**Free to play. Open in your browser. Choose your next world.**

## The library website

- **Nine illustrated game cards** with original embedded SVG cover artwork.
- **A featured adventure** introducing *The Fairest of All*.
- **Search** across game titles, descriptions, genres, and feature highlights.
- **Collection filters:** All adventures, Ages 8–12, Comedy, Craft & build, Sci-fi, and Dark & twisted.
- **Direct play links** to every game’s GitHub Pages site.
- **Age labels and content guidance**, including a visible mature-content notice for *Wonderrot*.
- **Responsive layouts** for phones, tablets, and desktop screens.
- **Accessibility features** including keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, a skip link, reduced-motion support, and an announced results count.
- **Fallback game links** when JavaScript is disabled.

The library is a single `index.html` file. Its styling, scripts, and cover illustrations are embedded, so there are no external fonts, image files, frameworks, or build tools to install. The games themselves are hosted separately and open through their respective links.

## The adventures

### The Fairest of All

**Fairy tale adventure · Choose-your-path RPG · Ages 8–12**

The Magic Mirror is vain, theatrical, and incapable of lying. It promises to make you the Fairest of All. You should probably read the fine print.

Become a hero in a world built from public-domain fairy tales. Explore the Briar Wood, climb the Glass Mountain, cross the Gingerbread Reach, and discover what waits beneath the Mirror’s promises. The screen itself is the mirror, reacting to your clock, browser tab, and touches without using a camera or microphone.

- 36 chapters across six acts.
- Hero progression from level 1 to 30.
- Classes, stats, gear, skills, shops, boss fights, achievements, and a map.
- Four endings and an after-credits sequel hook.
- Simple language, gentle scares, and a Leave panel that encourages breaks.

[Play The Fairest of All](https://drakedragon.github.io/Fairest/)

### Past Due

**Cosmic dungeon crawl · Dark comedy · A Notice-to-Vacate LitRPG**

The planet missed its rent. Your apartment building is now a dungeon. You are the night maintenance tech, and the whole universe is watching you try to get out.

Climb a ninety-nine-floor tower of absurd bureaucratic monsters while a sarcastic System narrator broadcasts your every move. Fight, loot, make friends, and uncover who owns the building—and what settling the account will really cost. A talking smoke detector named Chirp joins you on a climb that begins as a joke about paperwork and develops a heart of its own.

- 99 floors of story across 23 parts, with three endings.
- Branching choices and dice checks using Grit, Wits, Nerve, and Tinker.
- Turn-based combat, boss phases, loot, equipment upgrades, and floor-boss loot boxes.
- Party members, safe-room conversations, shops, and rest stops.
- A reactive livestream audience and over 230 achievements.
- Browser autosave, adjustable text size, and optional sound effects.

[Play Past Due](https://drakedragon.github.io/PastDue/)

### Pip and the Missing Endings

**Storybook adventure · Gentle fairy tale comedy · Ages 8–12**

On a rainy afternoon, you discover a glowing golden book in the attic. Out pops Pip: a tiny, monocled bookworm who snacks on boring words. A living ink Blot has been smudging the last page of every fairy tale, and Pip needs a reader brave, kind, or clever enough to restore their endings.

In **Book One: The Missing Endings**, explore the Merry Greenwood, the Yellow Road, the Enchanted Castle, and the Darkwood before climbing the Tower of Unfinished Stories. Meet familiar characters, make unlikely friends, and help the lonely Blot find an ending of his own.

In **Book Two: The Mixed-Up Pages**, Pip accidentally eats the Table of Contents. His hiccups scatter it into the wrong stories, sending you to the Beanstalk Kingdom, the Midnight Ball, the Three Pigs’ Village, and the Name Game before a finale in the Middle of the Book.

- Courage, Kindness, and Wits powers, with clearly displayed choice requirements.
- Five hearts and gentle setbacks: running out leads to a funny nap.
- Coins, shops, power boosts, and collectible Story Stars.
- Short passages, tap-to-continue reading, and a Back button.
- Four text sizes, light and dark themes, and an optional Read to me feature using your device’s voice.
- Automatic progress saving and 52 trophies with silly descriptions.

[Play Pip and the Missing Endings](https://drakedragon.github.io/MissingEndings/)

### HOTFIX

**Reality-editing LitRPG comedy · The Write Access series**

You are a regular person who finds a file that controls reality. You can edit it. Unfortunately, every edit has consequences, and the file is not well maintained.

Rewrite the world one configuration line at a time across four connected books:

| Book | Title | The situation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One | Write Access | Find the file and learn that Tuesdays can be edited. |
| Two | Merge Request | Your changes collide with everyone else’s, and a review is coming. |
| Three | Dependency Hell | Discover a reality built on packages, subscriptions, and a very tall tower. |
| Four | Final Release | End of Support is three days away. Close five blockers, say your goodbyes, and meet the Author. |

- Carry your character forward between books.
- Hundreds of branching scenes and consequences that echo into later events.
- Editable `reality.cfg` lines, relationships, allies, items, skills, and levels up to 25.
- Boss fights resolved through fighting, talking, or changing the rules.
- Drift and Heat meters with their own fail states.
- 39 endings across the series, including nine in the finale.
- Hidden achievements, a Records screen, and a write-your-own-last-line ending in every book.
- Adjustable text size and a dark terminal presentation.

[Play HOTFIX](https://drakedragon.github.io/HotFix/)

### The Crooked Crucible

**Crafting LitRPG · Gathering, commerce, and monster hunting**

You inherit a ruined forge, a sarcastic anvil, and ten crafts to master. Gather, smelt, weave, brew, enchant, and cook your way from humble beginnings to legendary equipment. Serve a town of particular customers, seek eight legendary relics, and test your creations against hundreds of monsters.

- Ten crafts: smithing, armoring, woodworking, weaving, jewelcrafting, arcane enchanting, alchemy, cooking, tools, and gadgets.
- 950 recipes across eleven material tiers, with quality grades up to Legendary.
- Craft levels up to 60 and hero progression up to level 80.
- Gathering zones, tools, apprentices, and offline catch-up.
- A living market, a rotating merchant, and 25 recurring commission customers.
- 37 hunting grounds and 185 monsters, including elites and bosses.
- Gear-based combat with weapon styles, elemental effects, enemy weaknesses, skills, and telegraphed attacks.
- Hunts, elite hunts, boss fights, and five-wave Gauntlets.

Progress is stored in your browser on each device. Use the game’s export and import tools to back up or transfer your save. A home-screen installation on iPhone can use separate storage from Safari; export your save before installing.

[Play The Crooked Crucible](https://drakedragon.github.io/Crooked_Crucible/)

### Wonderrot

**Psychological horror · Dark fantasy · Mature readers only**

> **Content warning:** violence, gore, psychological horror, and death.

Enter a dark, bloody Wonderland through a looking glass, guided by a fourth-wall-breaking Cheshire Cat. Choices carry forward, deaths leave Reflections, and familiar places become increasingly unsettling.

The adventure unfolds across six episodes:

1. The Long Fall
2. The Caterpillar’s Smoke
3. The Duchess’s Kitchen
4. The Mad Tea-Party
5. The Queen’s Croquet
6. The Trial

- Branching stories with replayable choices and Reflections that unlock new options.

A portable **Glass Code** carries progress between episodes. The episode library remembers and pre-fills it for the next adventure; players can also paste a code manually or enter a later episode as a stranger.

[Play Wonderrot](https://drakedragon.github.io/WonderRot/)

## Choosing an adventure

The collection includes different audiences and tones. **The Fairest of All**, **Pip and the Missing Endings**, and **Jingle Quest** are written for ages 8–12. **Wonderrot** is intended for mature readers and includes explicit horror content.

Other adventures feature themes such as dark comedy, combat, cosmic eviction, and reality-changing consequences. The library does not assign age ratings to those titles; review each game’s description before playing.

## Play and save progress

Select **Play adventure** on a game card to open its separate game website. The library is a navigation page; it does not store, transfer, or synchronize game saves.

Save behavior belongs to each individual game. Where a game offers export/import codes, use those tools to back up progress or move between devices. Browser-stored progress may be lost if site data is cleared or may be unavailable in a different browser or private session.

## Host the library on GitHub Pages

1. Create a repository for the collection library, separate from the game repositories if desired.
2. Upload `index.html` to the repository’s root. You can also upload this `README.md`.
3. Open **Settings → Pages** in the repository.
4. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
5. Choose the branch containing your files and the **/ (root)** folder, then save.
6. Once GitHub Pages finishes deploying, open the website address shown in the Pages settings.

No build command, package installation, or server configuration is required. Keep the filename exactly `index.html`.

## Customize the collection

Open `index.html` in a text editor. The `games` array near the bottom contains each game’s title, repository path, description, filters, highlights, and illustration settings. Edit those entries to update the collection.

Each game link follows this pattern:

```text
https://drakedragon.github.io/REPOSITORY_NAME/
```

Repository paths are case-sensitive. Preserve the correct spelling and capitalization when changing links. The navigation branding, introduction, featured game, and footer can also be edited directly in the HTML.
